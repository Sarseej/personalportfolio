# Visual system

Current direction: **The Latent Studio**. A computational observatory at blue hour, with a close three-quarter workstation in front of a glass wall and a deterministic landscape of projects, methods and experience.

Warm Aero is discarded as the active Home and Projects direction. Its stylesheet and material module remain unused cleanup candidates. Career and CV retain their existing document presentation and functionality pending a later visual phase. Historical decisions remain in the decision log.

`lib/visual/latent-studio.ts` owns scene colors, camera limits and flight duration. `app/latent-studio.css` supplies the corresponding named CSS palette. Night ink and architectural slate establish the room; smoked glass and blackened metal separate the monitors. Warm wood and an ivory CV paper receive amber task light. Lunar blue illuminates the exterior, with cyan reserved for project signals and muted ultraviolet for the mathematics cluster.

The workstation spans approximately 55% of the desktop viewport width. The foreground desk edge and chair, window mullions, multiple graph depths, low architectural silhouettes, horizon and blue-hour haze establish distance. Near dust has larger apparent parallax than the landscape because it occupies real foreground coordinates. The main monitor carries a factual OncoLA preview. The secondary monitor and physical CV remain independent destinations.

The identity stays large and readable above the workstation. A compact chapter index supplies independent Home, Projects, Career, CV and Contact access. Mobile uses a close crop, a two-line identity and at least 44px Home controls. The sound choice is a small translucent treatment over an already visible scene.

The landscape is deliberately abstract until Reveal structure exposes theme labels and selected project anchors. Lines express technology ownership, shared themes or the same project occurring in two supported themes. They do not represent embeddings, inferred similarity scores or model results. Repeated strands around shared-project links are a visual treatment of one relationship.

Projects becomes a full-viewport dark editorial chapter with oversized titles, a project index and a dominant project-specific system diagram. Existing facts, stage selection, technical expansions and limits remain readable without motion or sound.

Motion uses damped cursor response, 8–11 second cluster breathing, 14/18 second dust drift, occasional three-second connection pulses and a 24-second exterior light sweep. Reduced motion freezes decorative systems and cuts directly to destinations. Hidden tabs stop rendering and suspend audio.

Production posters are clean scene captures in `public`; review media belongs outside the repository. The current phase requires visual approval before later chapter conversion or historical-file cleanup.

## Signal field and editorial typography

The studio now opens into a dedicated signal field. Instrument Serif Regular carries the identity and editorial titles; Manrope carries reading and interface text; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for small technical metadata. Local Latin WOFF2 assets total 49,668 bytes, with SIL OFL notices. Display/body preload; metadata does not. Identity scale is `clamp(4.5rem, 10vw, 9.5rem)` at 0.92 line-height; mobile uses `clamp(58px, 17vw, 88px)`. Project titles use 48–96px; body text is 16px; controls generally 14px and metadata 12px.

The field uses three large project anchors, one outlined contribution anchor, ten softer concepts, and six precise technology points. Curves express 22 repository-supported relationships. Dashed relationships distinguish documented protocol decisions from implemented functionality. Overview limits labels; Connections exposes names, and selection gives concise relationship labels and source links. Mobile reserves relationship explanations for a bottom sheet.
