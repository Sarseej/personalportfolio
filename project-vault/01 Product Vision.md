# Product vision

An after-hours computational studio with cinematic evening light, walnut and midnight architectural surfaces. The desk is the navigation: main monitor → Projects, secondary monitor → Career, paper → CV. Preserve the accepted immersive studio, directed camera and semantic reading interfaces while improving furniture, materials and usability.

Sarseej Shrestha is an AI/ML developer and Computer Science student at Southeastern Louisiana University, with a B.S. expected May 2027. Positioning: “I build intelligent systems—and study what makes them trustworthy.” The body of work spans applied AI, medical machine learning, scientific computing and reliable data systems. No individual project defines the entire room.

The current design removes the former cultural theme and whiteboard. Personality comes from composition, carefully made furniture, restrained computational artwork, books and a secondary glass object. There is no standalone teaching destination. The continuous Mathematics Tutor → Senior Mathematics Tutor role appears in Career and CV, with a visible promotion milestone and no dedicated room object.

Projects answers what Sarseej has built. Career explains the chronological trajectory. CV provides conventional professional facts. Persistent Home, Projects, Career, CV and Contact navigation makes every destination obvious and immediately available. GitHub remains available in the entrance and CV.

No inference claims, fake terminals, operating-system imitation, unrestricted cameras, autoplay audio or navigation puzzles.
